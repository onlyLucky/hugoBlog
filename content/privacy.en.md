## What we collect

- **Local preferences**: Theme (light/dark) and similar settings are stored in browser `localStorage` and are not uploaded.
- **Server logs**: Hosting may keep standard access logs (IP, User-Agent, timestamp) for security and troubleshooting.
- **Analytics**: If Google Analytics or similar is enabled, it is used only to understand traffic and referrers. Personal data is not sold.

## What we do not do

- No account registration
- No sale or sharing of personally identifiable information
- No third-party ad tracking pixels

## Cookies

This site does not rely on advertising cookies. If analytics sets cookies, you can disable them in your browser.
